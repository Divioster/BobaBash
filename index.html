Reasons to Adopt a Fibi Chupi

A simple guide on why you need this tiny, sweet creature in your life.

Quick Facts

  • Size: Pocket-sized
  • Favorite Food: Strawberry pancakes & fresh berries
  • Language: Soft squeaks & “Chupi Chupi!”
  • Personality: Sweet, sleepy, and mildly chaotic

Top Reasons to Adopt Her

1. Perfectly Portable

She follows you around your room looking up at you with giant eyes. Fits right in your palm or jacket pocket.

2. Ultimate Stress Reliever

Her cheeks are soft like mochi. A gentle five-second cheek squish immediately melts away workday stress.

3. World Champion Sleeper

Curled up into a tiny sleepy bean on the duvet, she naps quietly so you can study or work in peace.

4. Instant Serotonin Lift

Hold her up toward the ceiling like baby Simba. She stretches her arms open with pure joy and squeaks.

5. Tucks In With Her Besties

She loves tucking under a chunky knit blanket alongside her mini acrylic standee and sleeping soundly.

6. Comforting Her Is Rewarding

When she accidentally snaps her wand, she cries big cartoon tears and just needs gentle headpats and some tape.

7. Huggable Big Plush Form

She also exists in a big soft plush form — great for holding on the couch while reading or watching shows.

8. Dramatic Sleep Positions

When her energy runs out, she flops flat on the floor with cute cross eyes (X_X) until recharged with a snack.

Daily Care Guide

  • Squish her cheeks at least three times a day for optimal softness.
  • Feed her fresh strawberry pancakes whenever she gives you the :3 face.
  • Lift her up high whenever you need an instant mood boost.
  • If her wand snaps, tape it back together immediately and say “good girl!”
